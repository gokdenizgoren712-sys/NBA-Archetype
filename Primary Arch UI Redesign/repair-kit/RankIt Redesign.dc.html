16 Craft references 4c · 4b · 4i
4cCrest system & compact anatomy
HOW A CLUB CREST SITS IN THE DIAMOND
The diamond is a frame, never the logo. Inside it a counter-rotated square at 62% of the diamond's side holds the real crest, object-fit: contain, upright. 62% is the largest square that clears the rotated corners with the radius applied. Three-letter type is the fallback when a club has no asset — same box, same optical centre, so a mixed list never looks broken.

STACKED PAIR
HOME IN FRONT
Centres sit 1.0 × the shield side apart — measured shield-to-shield, not on the rotated bounding box, which is 1.414× larger and the reason a naive left:0 / right:0 pair collapses on top of itself. Home paints in front with a 3px ring in the card's base colour, so the away crest reads as behind rather than merged. Container width is crest × 2.414 — anything tighter and the front shield’s edge crosses the rear label, which is a different bug from the pair collapsing. Never stack more than two.

17 Two P2s, and the surfaces stop disagreeing

The shield question was the last place mobile and web diverged, and the skin unlock was a condition 2j advertised with nothing behind it. Both close inside existing screens — neither earned a new one.

A DECISION REPLACED THREE INSTRUCTIONS
On 2r the shield question took the place of "Pick a match / give it stars / keep it." Those three steps were the product explaining itself, which the headline above them already does — and a first-run screen should spend its space on decisions, not instructions.
It ships on, with the reason stated rather than assumed: most people who watch late leave this on. A default with a reason is a recommendation; a default without one is a trap.
THE UNLOCK IS A ROW, NOT A MODAL
A skin unlocking is a consequence of the rating you just logged, so it belongs in the same list as the streak and the points — a fourth row on 6a with the skin's own thumbnail, the condition that met it, and USE IT.
A dedicated screen would have contradicted the rule that made 6a work: this product reports, it does not congratulate.
WHAT STAYS CUT
Season Wrapped is a turn of its own. Clips need a video pipeline nobody has. And group leaderboards would put popularity back into a product that removed follower counts on purpose.

9 People — how you reach one, and how you find one

3i is someone else's profile with a Following button on it — but nothing in the app led you there. 6b shows "184 following · 96 followers" as flat text with no destination, and no screen anywhere lets you find a person you don't already know. Two screens close it: the relationship list the counters point at, and the one place where finding someone is the job.

FOUR RELATIONSHIP STATES
One control, four states. It has to read the same in a list row and on a profile.
FOLLOWStranger. Gold, because it is the one action on that row.
FOLLOWINGAlready yours. Quiet — the work is done.
FOLLOW BACKThey found you first. Still gold — still an action.
MUTUALBoth directions. Never gold — nothing left to do.
TASTE OVERLAP IS THE HOOK
Every other app suggests people by mutuals or by who is popular. RankIt knows something better: how much you agree. 3i already computes it — 72%, "Deniz runs about half a star colder than you." That number is the reason to follow someone here, so it leads every suggestion on 9b rather than sitting on the profile as trivia.
A suggestion with fewer than 10 shared matches shows no percentage. An overlap computed from four matches is noise, and a wrong number here costs trust.
NO FOLLOWER COUNTS ON ROWS
A list row shows what someone watches — matches logged, classics, the overlap. Never how many followers they have. Popularity is not a reason to follow an opinion, and showing it turns a diary into a leaderboard.
