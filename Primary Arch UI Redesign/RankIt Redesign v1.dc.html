Built from mobile-screens.md (tokens + B-rules treated as binding; the Nba-Archetypes folder wasn't reachable from here). One thesis: today RankIt displays data about matches — after this pass, rating a match produces an artifact. The collectible stops being a card style and becomes the thing you earn, skin, hunt and share. Momento's lesson, applied to opinions instead of attendance.

How good the match was, cold to incandescent. Routed through indigo and magenta on purpose, so no step ever reads as gold.
