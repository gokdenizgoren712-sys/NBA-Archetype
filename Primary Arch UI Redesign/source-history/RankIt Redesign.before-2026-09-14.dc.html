4cCrest system & compact anatomy
HOW A CLUB CREST SITS IN THE DIAMOND
The diamond is a frame, never the logo. Inside it a counter-rotated square at 62% of the diamond's side holds the real crest, object-fit: contain, upright. 62% is the largest square that clears the rotated corners with the radius applied. Three-letter type is the fallback when a club has no asset — same box, same optical centre, so a mixed list never looks broken.

STACKED PAIR
HOME IN FRONT
Centres sit 1.0 × the shield side apart — measured shield-to-shield, not on the rotated bounding box, which is 1.414× larger and the reason a naive left:0 / right:0 pair collapses on top of itself. Home paints in front with a 3px ring in the card's base colour, so the away crest reads as behind rather than merged. Container width is crest × 2.414 — anything tighter and the front shield’s edge crosses the rear label, which is a different bug from the pair collapsing. Never stack more than two.

WHY IT STOPPED COLLIDING
The wide card puts crest · score · crest in one row, so at 155px the three fight over ~50px each. Compact gives the score its own line and the crests their own line. Same card, same notches, same heat row — different reading order.

IT WAS NEVER WIRED UP
mipmap-anydpi-v26/ic_launcher.xml points its foreground at @mipmap/ic_launcher_foreground — the stock Android robot — over a white background. No Primary Arch mark exists anywhere in the repo — that one is genuinely new.

Nothing invented. The silhouette is the collectible card itself — the same chamfer, cut top-right and bottom-left — and the star knocked out of it is the app's own rating glyph. Approved: inset 1 — applied to every specimen above and in the app. On dark ground all three work and full width is arguably the boldest. On the busy ground the difference is decisive: at full width the teal caps land exactly on the ring's left and right vertices, so the outline reads as cut and the mark loses its closure against competing tone. One unit of inset puts the caps just inside the vertices — the ring stays a closed twelve-gon, and the rule still overhangs the seams enough to read as passing through the ball. Inset 2 closes the ring but the rule stops feeling like it crosses anything. Your call whether it's worth touching a shipped mark; if you'd rather not, keep full width and never place it on photography.

Stroke 4 on 48 is 1/12 of the width, chunky and right. But at 16px it renders 1.33px with the seams ~2px off the ring. 24px is the floor for the stroke form. Below it, the solid construction from note 1 takes over rather than dropping the seams. App icon gets drawn at 108, never scaled from 48.

Twelve screens, zero additions to the palette. Everything reuses ink, heat, gold and the club vars.
